# Payroll Policy

Company: Cedar Health (fictional)
Version: 1

This document contains fictional policy information for a learning project.

## PAYROLL-001: Salary payment date

Monthly salary is paid on the last working day of each month.

## PAYROLL-002: Payslips and payroll questions

Employees can download their monthly payslips from the employee portal
after their salary has been paid.

Employees should contact the payroll team by email if a payslip is missing
or they have a question about a salary payment.
