# Notice Period Policy

Company: Cedar Health (fictional)
Version: 1

This document contains fictional policy information for a learning project.

## NOTICE-001: Notice period

Full-time employees must serve a notice period of 30 calendar days
when resigning.

## NOTICE-002: Submitting a resignation

Employees must send their resignation by email to their reporting
manager and the HR team.

The HR team confirms the employee's final working day by email.
